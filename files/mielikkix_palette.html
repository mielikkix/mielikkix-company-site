Three Palette Directions

A side-by-side comparison of the three color systems, each paired with its intended usage and a logo concept — for picking the direction to build the brand on.

Palette 1 — Bold, High-Tech & Mystical

Forest & Aurora

Deep, infinite forest tones with a neon AI pulse — built to make bright accents feel like data lighting up in the dark.

Deep Forest
#0F2A1F
Primary background — the site's main canvas, infinite and dark so bright accents pop.
Aurora Neon
#00E5A0
Primary accent — CTA buttons, hover states, AI-powered data visualizations.
Antler Gold
#D4AF37
Secondary accent — logo mark, badges, premium service highlights.
Mist White
#E8F0EB
Text & icons — soft frosty white for body copy on dark backgrounds.

#4A3525

Honey Glow
#F2C94C
Primary accent — logo symbol and primary buttons; trust, happiness, approachability.
Pine Needle
#1E3A2F
Primary background — a softer dark green for the main site canvas.
Creamy Linen
#FDFAF3
Text & cards — warm off-white for article cards, testimonials, body text.

Palette 3 — Ethereal, Premium & Minimalist

Mist & Foxfire

A high-end, SaaS-style AI brand — quiet, focused, and intuitive, relying on contrast rather than loud color.

Midnight Pine
#1D2B2A
Primary background — a very dark, desaturated teal-green.
Sage Mist
#9BB0A6
Secondary elements — borders, subheadings, less prominent UI.
Foxfire Orange
#FF6B3D
Primary accent — used sparingly for logo mark, sign-up buttons, active states.
Arctic Ice
#F4F9F7
Primary text — pure, crisp white for headlines against the dark background.
